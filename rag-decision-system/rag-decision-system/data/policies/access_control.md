# Access Control Policy

## Principles
Access to company systems must follow the principle of least privilege: each
person receives only the access required for their role. Access requests
require manager approval before they are granted.

## Requirements
Administrative access requires multi-factor authentication (MFA) at all
times. Shared accounts are prohibited: every user must have a unique account
so actions can be attributed to an individual.

## Reviews
Access rights are reviewed quarterly. Access that is no longer required for
a role must be removed within five business days of the review.

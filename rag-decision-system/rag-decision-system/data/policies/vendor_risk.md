# Vendor Risk Policy

## Before sharing data with a vendor
An external vendor or third party may only receive company or customer data
after a security review has been completed and a data processing agreement
(DPA) has been signed. Sharing data with a vendor without a security review
or without a signed DPA is prohibited.

## Ongoing requirements
Vendors with access to customer data are reassessed annually. Vendor access
must be removed when the contract ends.

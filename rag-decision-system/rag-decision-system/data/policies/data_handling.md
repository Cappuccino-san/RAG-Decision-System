# Data Handling Policy

## Encryption
Customer data and personal data must be encrypted at rest and in transit.
Sending customer data without encryption is prohibited.

## Storage
Customer data may only be stored in approved company systems. Storing
customer data on personal drives, personal cloud accounts, or unmanaged
devices is prohibited.

## Sharing
Customer data must never be sent to an external party except under the
Vendor Risk Policy, after a security review and a signed data processing
agreement are in place.
